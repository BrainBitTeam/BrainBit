radio_command.py:

1. Increase the ALSA Buffer Size
--buffer-size=32768: Increases the amount of audio stored in the hardware buffer.


arecord -D hw:1,0 -f S16_LE -r 16000 -c 1 -t raw --buffer-size=32768 | python3 -u /home/root/radio_command.py


command with delay for radio_command with hotword
(sleep 2; arecord -D hw:1,0 -f S16_LE -r 16000 -c 1 -t raw -B 1000000) | python3 -u /home/root/radio_command_w_wake_word.py

shorter delay
